名詞與方法
股票範圍
主要掛牌在 NYSE（含 NYSE American，合併列在 NYSE）或 Nasdaq 的普通股，以 — 有成交的為準。不含 ETF、ADR／存託憑證（依 Nasdaq 官方證券名稱判斷）、特別股、權證、SPAC 單位。同一家公司有多種股票（例如 GOOGL／GOOG）只列一行：取最近約 60 個交易日成交金額較大的那一種，市值用公司全部股數計算。
跌幅
— 收盤價相對今年收盤最高點的變化。價格已還原現金配息與股票分割，所以高點會和看盤軟體上的原始價格不同。跌幅以未四捨五入的價格計算，可能和用顯示數字算出的差一點。
部門與產業（TradingView，非官方）
美國交易所沒有官方的產業分類。本頁採用 TradingView 網站上的分類（由 FactSet 依公司主要營收來源分類），上方表格用「部門」，名單的「產業」欄是更細的分類。每家公司只有一個分類，和市場常說的「概念股」不同。產業名稱後的「†」= TradingView 中文頁面沒有取到，由英文名稱翻譯。「未分類」= TradingView 沒有提供分類。
市值
美國證管會（SEC）財報封面上的流通股數 × — 收盤價，單位億美元。股數隨季報／年報更新，可能落後一季；之後若有股票分割會依比例調整。沒有申報全公司股數的公司（常見於有多種股票或外國公司），改用 Massive 提供的股數，市值前標「≈」。
EPS 與本益比
EPS = SEC 財報（10-Q／10-K）裡近四季稀釋每股盈餘的合計（GAAP，非公司自行調整的數字）；第四季由全年減前三季推得。資料下載日 —。本益比 = — 收盤 ÷ EPS；超過 100 倍顯示「＞100」。近四季合計為零或負（虧損）時顯示「—」；外國公司（只交年報或採 IFRS）、剛上市公司常算不出近四季，也顯示「—」；近四季內有股票分割的公司，EPS 會混用分割前後的數字，也暫不顯示。EPS 後的「ᶠ」= 改用最近一個完整會計年度。本益比高低本身沒有好壞，不同產業的常見水準也不一樣；本益比篩選只影響完整名單，不影響上方的檔數摘要與部門表。
排名與排名變動
排名：同一交易所、市值 150 億美元以上的股票依跌幅由大到小排，1 = 跌最多。排名變動：和 — 的排名比，▲ = 排名往前（相對跌得更多），▼ = 往後，「-」= 不變。
●（本週新跌破門檻）
— 時跌幅還沒到你設定的「跌幅至少」，— 到了。和高點在哪天無關，也不代表今年第一次跌到這個程度；門檻改了，● 也會跟著變。跌幅下方的小字是 — 的跌幅，方便對照。
標記
「今年新掛牌或改代號」：今年 1/2 之後才出現在價格資料裡，高點常在掛牌初期；改過代號的公司也會被標，跌幅只從新代號開始算。「含單日變動超過 40% 的日子」：還原後仍有單日漲跌超過 40%，可能是真實的大波動，也可能是未還原的公司行動（例如分拆），跌幅請保守看待。
Terms and method
Universe
Common stocks whose primary listing is on the NYSE (NYSE American is included and shown under NYSE) or Nasdaq, that traded on —. ETFs, ADRs/depositary receipts (judged by Nasdaq's official security names), preferreds, warrants and SPAC units are excluded. A company with several share classes (e.g. GOOGL/GOOG) appears once: the class with the higher dollar volume over about the last 60 trading days, with market cap based on all of the company's shares.
Drop
Change of the — close against this year's highest close. Prices are adjusted for cash dividends and splits, so the high differs from unadjusted chart prices. Drops are computed from unrounded prices and may differ slightly from the displayed figures.
Sector and industry (TradingView, non-official)
US exchanges do not publish an official industry classification. This page uses TradingView's classification (FactSet, based on each company's main source of revenue): the table above uses the sector; the list's "Industry" column is the finer level. Each company has one classification only, unlike market "themes". "Unclassified" = TradingView gives no classification.
Market cap
Shares outstanding from the cover page of SEC filings × the — close, in US$ bn. Share counts update with quarterly and annual reports and may lag by a quarter; later stock splits are applied. Where a company does not report a company-wide share count (often multi-class or foreign companies), the share count from Massive is used and the market cap is marked "≈".
EPS and P/E
EPS = sum of diluted EPS over the last four quarters from SEC filings (10-Q/10-K, GAAP, not company-adjusted figures); the fourth quarter is derived as full year minus the first three. Downloaded —. P/E = — close ÷ EPS; above 100× shows ">100". "—" when the four-quarter total is zero or negative (a loss); foreign companies (annual reports only, or IFRS) and recent listings often have no four-quarter figure and also show "—"; companies with a stock split inside the four quarters are not shown because their reported EPS mixes pre- and post-split figures. "ᶠ" after EPS = latest full fiscal year used instead. A high or low P/E is not good or bad in itself, and typical levels differ by industry. The P/E filter affects the full list only, not the counts or the sector table.
Rank and rank change
Rank: stocks on the same exchange with market cap ≥ US$15 bn, ordered by drop, 1 = fell most. Rank change: against —; ▲ = moved up (fell more than others), ▼ = moved down, "-" = unchanged.
● (newly past the threshold)
On — the drop had not yet reached your "Drop at least" setting; on — it has. It says nothing about when the high was, and does not mean this is the first time this year the stock fell this far; it follows the threshold you choose. The small figure under the drop is the drop on —.
Flags
"New listing or ticker change this year": first appears in the price data after 2 January; the high is often set in the first days of trading, and for a ticker change the drop only covers the new ticker. "Has a day with a move over 40%": after adjustment there is still a one-day move above 40%. It may be a real move or an unadjusted corporate action (e.g. a spin-off), so read the drop with care.
